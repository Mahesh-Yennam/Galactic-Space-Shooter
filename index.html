🚀 Flight Manual & Power-Ups

⚡
Triple Blaster: Upgrades ship cannons to a 3-way plasma spread for 8s.
🛡️
Force Shield: Projects an energy bubble absorbing 1 collision or laser blast.
💣
EMP Shockwave: Screen-clearing bomb that obliterates all standard targets.
🔧
Hull Repair Core: Restores +1 starship hull integrity point.
🪨
Asteroid Belts: Durable rocky obstacles that shatter when blasted (+30 Pts).
👾
Alien Mothership: Heavy boss flagship appearing periodically (+800 Pts).

Desktop Controls: Use Arrow Keys or WASD to steer your ship. Spacebar to blast lasers. You can also simply hover and drag your Mouse across the canvas to glide smoothly! Press P to pause.
Mobile Controls: Tap or hold the on-screen Directional D-Pad + FIRE button, or drag your finger directly across the screen.
